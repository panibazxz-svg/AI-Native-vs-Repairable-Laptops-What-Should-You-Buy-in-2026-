AI-Native vs Repairable Laptops: What Should You Buy in 2026?
AI-capable laptops and repairable laptops address two different buying priorities. AI-focused models include dedicated hardware such as a neural processing unit (NPU) to accelerate supported on-device tasks. Repairable laptops, meanwhile, are designed to make components such as batteries, keyboards, storage, and other parts easier to replace.

You do not necessarily have to choose one priority over the other. Some laptops combine modern AI hardware with replaceable components, while others prioritize a thin design or maximum performance. If you want a laptop that remains useful for several years, compare its actual software requirements, memory capacity, repair options, spare-part availability, and total ownership cost rather than relying on AI branding alone.

With a July 31, 2026 compliance date requiring seven to ten years of maintenance service and parts availability, the EU’s right-to-repair directive offered manufacturers a compelling reason to be concerned.
